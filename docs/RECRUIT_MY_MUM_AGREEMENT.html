2. Purpose

2.1 HumanMetrix provides AI-powered candidate and workforce assessments (including FIT, AI Readiness, Prompt Architecture and AIQ assessments) and related reporting and analysis tools via its online platform (the "Platform").

2.2 The Client wishes to purchase prepaid assessment credits ("Credits") to use the Platform for its recruitment and talent-assessment activities, on the terms set out in this Agreement.

3. Commercial Terms

Price per Credit: ZAR 25.00 (twenty-five South African Rand), excluding any applicable taxes.
Bonus Credits: For every 500 Credits purchased in a single order, the Client receives 30 additional Credits free of charge.
Set-up Cost: There is no upfront set-up cost, onboarding fee, licence fee or platform-access fee of any kind.

3.1 Prepaid purchase. Credits are purchased upfront. Credits are credited to the Client's Platform account promptly upon receipt of cleared payment.

3.2 Bonus mechanics. Bonus Credits are calculated per whole block of 500 purchased Credits within a single order (e.g. an order of 500 Credits yields 530 Credits in total; an order of 1,000 Credits yields 1,060 Credits in total). Bonus Credits carry the same usage rights as purchased Credits but hold no monetary value and are non-refundable.

3.3 No set-up fees. For the avoidance of doubt, HumanMetrix shall not charge the Client any upfront set-up, implementation, integration, onboarding or subscription fee under this Agreement. The Client's only payment obligation is for Credits purchased under clause 3.1.

3.4 Price protection. The per-Credit price in this clause applies to all Credit purchases made during the Initial Term. Any price change thereafter requires at least 30 days' written notice and applies only to purchases made after the notice period.

4. Payment

4.1 Invoices are payable within [14/30] days of the invoice date, in South African Rand, by bank transfer or by any payment method made available through the Platform.

4.2 Prices exclude VAT and any other applicable taxes, duties or levies, which shall be added at the prevailing rate where required by law.

4.3 Credits are non-transferable to third parties and, save as required by applicable law, purchased Credits are non-refundable once consumed.

5. Use of Credits & Platform Access

5.1 Credits are consumed when the Client initiates assessments, analyses or other credit-based features on the Platform, at the credit rates displayed in the Platform at the time of use.

5.2 Credits do not expire during the term of this Agreement. On termination, unused purchased Credits remain usable for 90 days or, at the Client's election, the unused purchased (non-bonus) balance shall be refunded pro rata.

5.3 HumanMetrix grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Platform for its internal recruitment and talent-assessment purposes during the term. Use of the Platform is also subject to HumanMetrix's End User License Agreement, which applies to the extent not inconsistent with this Agreement (in which case this Agreement prevails).

6. Data Protection

6.1 Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law, including (where applicable) the South African Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR, in respect of personal information of candidates processed under this Agreement.

6.2 As between the parties, the Client is the responsible party / controller for candidate personal information it submits, and HumanMetrix processes such information as operator / processor solely to deliver the assessment services, in accordance with its published privacy policy and documented instructions of the Client.

6.3 The Client warrants that it has a lawful basis (including candidate consent where required) for submitting candidate information to the Platform.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other in connection with this Agreement and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, except where disclosure is required by law or the information is already lawfully public.

8. Term & Termination

8.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 12 months (the "Initial Term"), renewing automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives at least 30 days' written notice of non-renewal.

8.2 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach that remains unremedied 30 days after written notice, or becomes insolvent.

9. Liability

9.1 Neither party excludes liability for fraud, gross negligence or wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be excluded by applicable law.

9.2 Subject to clause 9.1, each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the total amount paid by the Client for Credits in the 12 months preceding the claim, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

9.3 Assessment outputs are decision-support information only; hiring and employment decisions remain the sole responsibility of the Client.
