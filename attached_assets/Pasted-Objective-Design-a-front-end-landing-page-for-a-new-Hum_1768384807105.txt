Objective
Design a front-end landing page for a new HumanMetrix service called “Team-Fit Diagnostic.”
The page should explain the concept clearly to employers (HR leaders, founders, executives), guide them through how the service works, and make the value of team-level capability analysis immediately understandable.

This is not a personality test. The page must emphasize capability balance at the team level and practical hiring and team-design outcomes.

Target Audience

Employers

HR / People teams

Founders and leadership teams

Organizations struggling with talented teams that still fail

Core Message

Teams don’t fail because of the wrong personalities.
They fail because critical human capabilities are unevenly distributed.

HumanMetrix Team-Fit diagnoses how human capabilities are distributed across a team, identifies gaps and overload risks, and recommends how to rebalance teams through hiring or internal changes.

Capabilities to Highlight

Show clearly and consistently throughout the page:

HA — High Agency

IC — Intellectual Curiosity

RA — Resilience

EE — Empathy & Ethics

GM — Growth Mindset

IA — Interpersonal Agility

Frame these as complementary capabilities, not traits or roles.

Page Structure & Content Requirements
1. Hero Section

Headline focused on designing teams that actually work

Sub-headline explaining team systems, not personality tests

Primary CTA: Start Team Diagnostic

Secondary CTA: View Sample Report

Short credibility line (scenario-based, team-level, modern work)

2. Problem → Solution

Problem

Talented teams still struggle

Hiring “more of the same” doesn’t fix team gaps

Personality/role tests don’t diagnose system-level issues

Solution

HumanMetrix analyzes team capability distribution

Reveals gaps, overload risks, and imbalance patterns

Enables targeted hiring and team design decisions

3. How It Works (4-Step Flow)

Step 1: Define Organization Context

Company size

Industry & sub-industry

Team type

Work mode (remote / hybrid / on-site)

Optional mission or ethos text

Step 2: Add Team Members

Upload existing HumanMetrix reports (PDFs)

Or send assessment links to team members

Step 3: Generate Team Report

System analyzes team-level capability patterns

Client waits for report generation

Step 4: Take Action

Use insights to hire, rebalance, or develop the team

4. What the Analysis Does (Behind the Scenes)

Clearly explain that the system:

Maps capability coverage across the team

Identifies concentration & overload risks

Detects imbalance patterns (e.g. high execution, low ethics)

Compares the team against goal-specific profiles (innovation, stability, ethics, speed)

Simulates ideal complement hires based on missing capabilities

Avoid technical jargon; keep it outcome-focused.

5. What the Client Receives

Present as a clean list or grid:

Team Capability Map (distribution, not averages)

Overload & bottleneck alerts

Risk & imbalance analysis

Goal alignment assessment

Ideal complement hire profiles

Downloadable team report

6. Differentiation Section

Contrast HumanMetrix with:

Personality tests

Role-based models

Key differentiators:

Scenario-based, behavioral measurement

Team-system diagnostics (not individual labels)

Designed for modern, AI-era work

Actionable hiring and team design recommendations

Privacy-first (team-level by default)

7. Sample Report Preview

Include a visible or revealable mock preview that shows:

Capability distribution across the six skills

Highlighted risks or gaps

Example hiring recommendation (“Ideal complement profile”)

This helps employers visualize the outcome.

8. Final Call to Action

Clear closing message about seeing hidden team gaps

CTA: Start Team Diagnostic

Small note that this is an early / front-end experience if relevant

Tone & Style Guidelines

Professional, calm, and credible

Evidence-informed (but not academic on the page)

Avoid absolute claims or guarantees

Emphasize insight, risk signals, and better decision-making

Position HumanMetrix as a team diagnostic system, not HR fluff

Output Expectation
Design the page content, structure, and flow so it can later be implemented in front-end code. Focus on clarity, narrative, and employer relevance — not technical implementation.
